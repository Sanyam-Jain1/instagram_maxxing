# Channel direction: working notes

Status: undecided. The pipeline exists (see CLAUDE.md); the niche and animation style do not.
Next step is to watch the reference reels below, then pick two concepts to pilot.

## Two decisions

1. **Content niche**: factual/informative, stories, "what if", interactive series, mythology.
2. **Animation style**: geometric characters, clay-shaped characters, or blocky Roblox-like characters.

More than one niche, or more than one channel, is fine. The goal is something distinctive
that can go viral, not a generic account.

## What the pipeline can produce

- **Code-rendered today or with modest work**: animated text, flat geometric characters, maps,
  counters, chat/app mock-ups, real simulations.
- **A real build**: blocky or clay-looking 3D characters (three.js). Use an original blocky
  style, not Roblox's own look.
- **Needs an AI video/image tool that is not set up**: the fully AI-generated look of
  `DfhMN7miUDV` and `DfwnvqGpO6O`.

## Concepts proposed so far

1. **The comments run this village.** 100 shape-people in a real simulation. The top comment
   becomes a law; the next reel shows the computed consequences. "Day 9. You voted to ban
   sleep. 31 villagers remain." Strongest engagement loop, hardest to copy.
2. **What if, India: answered seriously.** Absurd premises worked through with deadpan maths,
   maps and counters. "What if every Indian jumped at the same time?"
3. **Brainrot University.** Dumb shape-character stories that turn out, in the last three
   seconds, to be a real paradox or idea (a samosa sues a triangle; it was the Ship of Theseus).
4. **18 Days.** The Kurukshetra war, one reel per day, as an animated battle map of the
   vyuhas. Stick to the source text, cite it, never play it for laughs.
5. **Tiny fables.** Calm cream-and-serif 25-second parables with shape characters and a
   one-line moral.

Recommendation given: (1) as the flagship, (2) as a second channel, (3) as the third test.
One format per channel for its first 30 posts.
